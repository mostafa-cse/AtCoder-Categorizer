## Key Invariants & Techniques
- Invariants: The expected stopping time on the cycle Z / 10^9 Z is an absorbing Markov chain with rational generating function E(x) = P(x) / Q(x). Factoring the denominator over the algebraic closure reveals geometric modes, a double root at 1 corresponding to steady-state linear drift, and conjugate root pairs handled by Bostan-Mori doubling of the rational recurrence.
- Common Reductions: Find the expected number of rolls of a fair 6-sided die until the cumulative sum satisfies X = R mod 10^9, modulo 998244353.
